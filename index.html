Australia’s visitor economy is rebuilding — but not evenly

Tourism is one of Australia's largest and most influential industries, connecting domestic travel, international aviation, hospitality, education, and regional economies into a single visitor network. Every year, millions of Australians travel interstate while international visitors arrive from major global markets across Asia, Europe, and North America. Together, these journeys generate billions in visitor spending and support hundreds of thousands of jobs nationwide. Yet the industry was transformed by the COVID-19 pandemic, which triggered the sharpest collapse in modern tourism history. While travel demand has returned strongly since borders reopened, the recovery has been uneven across states, cities, and international markets. Some destinations have surpassed pre-pandemic levels, while others continue to rely on the slow return of high-value overseas visitors.

Data sources: Tourism Research Australia (TRA) National Visitor Survey (NVS) and International Visitor Survey (IVS) · Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) Overseas Arrivals & Departures · Year ending December 2024 ·
